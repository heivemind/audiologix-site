Common questions

How do I bring in a song?
Tap an empty record on the Deck, or "Bring in any audio" in the Riff Lab or Riff Library. You can pick songs from your phone, any file, the sound from a video or a download link. You can also share a file to AudioLOGIX from another app.

Why can't I load songs from Spotify or Apple Music?
Streaming services protect their songs, so no other app can open them. Songs you bought, synced or saved as files work.

Where are my recordings?
Every synth take is in Tapes: the music button at the top right of the Noise tab. A copy is also kept in your phone's Files app: on iPhone under On My iPhone › AudioLOGIX › tapes, on Android under Music › AudioLOGIX.

The tuner doesn't react.
Make sure AudioLOGIX is allowed to use the microphone in your phone's Settings.

Can I see a guide again?
Yes. Open Options (the gear, top right) and replay any guide.
