AUDIOLOGIX Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

AudioLOGIX is a music app: synths, DJ decks and guitar tools. In short, everything stays on your phone. The app has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and it doesn't collect, sell or share any of your information.

Microphone

The tuner, the riff recorder, the oscilloscope and the Guitar Synth listen through the microphone, and only while those screens are open. The sound is processed on your phone. It is never uploaded or sent anywhere. Recordings you choose to keep are saved in the app on your phone.

Your music and files

When you load a song onto the decks, the Riff Lab or the Riff Library, AudioLOGIX reads the song you pick from your phone's music library, your files or a video. It makes a working copy inside the app. Nothing you load is uploaded. On iPhone the app asks to see your music library so it can list your songs. On Android it asks for "Music and audio" access for the same reason.

Download links

If you paste a download link (for example a Dropbox or Google Drive share link), the app downloads that one file straight from that service. That service sees an ordinary download request, as it would from a web browser. AudioLOGIX sends nothing else.

Sharing

If you use the Share button on a take or riff, the file goes only where you send it, through your phone's own share sheet.

Data stored on your phone

Practice history, settings, riffs, takes and imported audio are stored only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them. AudioLOGIX has no servers and keeps no copy.

Children

AudioLOGIX collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
